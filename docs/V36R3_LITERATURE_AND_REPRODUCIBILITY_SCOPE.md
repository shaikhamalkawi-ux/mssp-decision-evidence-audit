The contribution is a traceable, source- and target-specific audit of **four linked but logically distinct planning questions**: package component predicates, authorized priorities, identifiable financial allocations, and dated service-launch evidence. The combination reveals where one type of record supports only a weaker claim. It is not proposed as a new general identification theorem, assurance formalism, proven operational deployment or performance-superior method.
