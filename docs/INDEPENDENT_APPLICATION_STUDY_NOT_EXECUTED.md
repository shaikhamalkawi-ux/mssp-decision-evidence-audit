# Prospective human application assessment (NOT EXECUTED)

**Status:** protocol only. No reviewer has been recruited, no agreement score measured, and no author-approved field validation exists.

Precondition: source excerpts must be independently permission-cleared, with common version, context, and redaction rules. Two individuals who did not construct the casebook independently classify a prespecified source claim set, which must include positives, conflicts, admission-holds, bounded/nonidentified targets, and absent-outcome cases. Separate the **eight frozen paper casebook examples** from any later hold-out set; do not imply the examples themselves are held out.

Record claim origin/intended use, unit, time, evidence admit/hold, value/status, witness, evidence request, and time, *without* giving judges the author casebook conclusions. Declare a consensus/adjudication process after independent decisions, including disagreements and uncertainties; avoid reporting an agreement statistic without a defensible coding ontology and denominator. Report both interpretive agreement and substantive reasoning. This would be a single-case portability test, not cross-company validation.
