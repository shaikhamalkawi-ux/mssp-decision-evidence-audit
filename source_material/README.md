## Structure and scientific scope

- `draft2/`: proposal dashboard workbook and two matching PDF documents, dated 26 June 2026.
- `draft3/`: financial workbook, executive presentation and final planning report (source folder 30 June 2026; related PDFs dated 2–3 July 2026). The controlling planning scope excludes KSA and Kuwait. The financial workbook also retains a legacy sheet with broader geography; do not confuse them.
- `consolidation/`: the 25-package mapping workbook and its two source PDF renderings.

**Year 1, Year 2 and Year 3 are prospective planning columns** in these source files, not three independent historical annual account datasets. Values represent financial, customer and service targets/scenarios. They are not verified revenue, implementation, client acceptance, staff time savings, cybersecurity effectiveness, or profit.

Draft 2 and Draft 3 describe *different portfolio/geographic assumptions*; directly interpreting their Year-3 amount difference as growth would be incorrect. The research should be evaluated using the version-matched source workbook/PDF pair for each claim.

The selection excludes separate weekly-reflection submissions, personal event photos, duplicate PDF copies, PowerPoint/Word authoring variants, and the empty Draft 1 inaccessible-export placeholder. No claims about those omitted objects are made.
